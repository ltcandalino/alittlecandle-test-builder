# Exam formatting rules

Read from Leigh's *Geometry Unit 4 Exam Transformations v1 SY2627* (Word settings, checked against the 6-page PDF),
2026-11-24, with her answers the same day. **Not in the app yet.** For tests and quizzes, in the preview, Print/PDF, Word and
LaTeX. Measurements are Word's own numbers; **Decided** marks where her answer replaces what that exam does.

## 1. Page

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper | Letter, 8.5 × 11 in, portrait, one column |
| Top / bottom margin | 1 in |
| Left / right margin | 0.75 in (text is 7 in wide) |
| Header / footer distance from the edge | 0.5 in |
| First page | different header and footer from the other pages |

**Decided 2026-11-24:** tests and quizzes use these margins. Reviews and lessons stay at 0.5 in.

## 2. Type

- Century Schoolbook 12 pt, black, for everything except where this file says otherwise.
- Math: Word equations, Cambria Math 12 pt (letters italic, Δ and numbers upright).
- **Decided:** triangles print as Δ (ΔABC), not △, on every test and quiz. The banks keep △.
- *Not* in a question is italic. Directions to the student inside a question are italic
  (*Explain your reasoning.*, [*Leave all construction marks.*]).

**Spacing, decided 2026-11-24** (replaces 1.15 everywhere): question text single, choices that are stacked or 2 × 2 at 1.15,
0 before and after.

## 3. Headers and footers

**Line at the top of every page:** course name, bold, centered, 1.5 pt line under it across the page. The line starts
0.125 in left of the text.

**First page header, in this order:**
1. Course name and line.
2. A blank line.
3. `Name:` + 37 underscores on the left; `Date:` + 17 underscores ending at the right margin. Regular weight.
4. `Quarter 2 – Unit 4 Exam` bold at the left; unit title (`Transformations`) **bold** at the right margin. 1.15 line.
5. A blank line, then teacher (`Candalino`) left, calculator (`TI-84`) middle, `1.5x/2.0x/_____ minutes left` right; all
   italic. **Decided:** the whole line prints only on the
   accommodated copy; regular versions have nothing under the title.

**Other pages:** item 1 only.

**Footers:** first page `v. 1`, left, 10 pt gray (#808080). Other pages: page number, right, 12 pt, no words.

## 4. Parts

- Name: `Part I`, `Part II`… bold, centered, single spaced.
- Directions: bold, left, single spaced, 0 before and after.
- Part total `[14]`: bold, right. **Decided:** at the right margin on the last line of the directions when it fits,
  otherwise on its own line at the right margin.
- One blank line, then the questions (that exam skips it in Part IV).
- Each part starts on a new page. **Decided:** a checkbox on Set up, on to begin with.
- Question numbers continue through all parts (1–18).

| Part | Contents | Credits each | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | fill in the blank / short answer | 1 | [6] |
| II | multiple choice | 2 | [14] |
| III | open response | 2 | [8] |
| IV | open response with parts (a), (b), (c) | 4 | [4] |

## 5. Short answer (Part I): no table

- Word numbered list, number bold (`1.`), text indented 0.25 in (hanging 0.25 in).
- Spacing 0 before, 0 after; single (this exam has 1.15 on the questions with math).
- Two blank lines after a fill-in question, three after one that needs work.
- Blanks are underscores inside the sentence: 16–18 for a word, 25 at the end of a sentence, 5 for a number.
- A diagram goes under its question at the left margin (about 2.85 × 2.9 in).

## 6. Multiple choice (Part II): borderless table

**Table:** no borders, 6.3 in wide, at the left margin; cells have 0.075 in left and right padding and none above or below.

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Empty strip to the right |
|---|---|---|
| 0.4 in: the question number only | 5.9 in: everything else | 0.7 in to the margin, for work |

- One row per question. Row height grows with its content.
- Number: same numbered list as Part I, bold, 0.25 in hanging.
- Question text: 12 pt, single spaced, 0 before and after, left aligned.
- One blank line between the question and its choices.
- Two blank lines after the last choice (more when students need room or a picture is beside the choices).

**Choices:** lowercase letters, bold, with a period (`a.`), 0.25 in hanging indent, restarting at a. for each question.

| Choices are | Layout | Line spacing |
|---|---|---|
| very short (numbers, coordinates, a word) | one row of four: borderless table inside column 2, four equal columns, about 1.44 in each | single |
| short equations | 2 × 2: borderless table inside column 2, two equal columns, about 2.87 in each; a b / c d across | 1.15 (this exam has 1.5, then 1.15) |
| sentences | one under another, no inner table | 1.15 |

**Pictures. Decided:** automatic, no setting.
- Beside the choices, at the right, when the choices are stacked sentences and the picture is at most 2.5 in wide and roughly
  square. The choices then stop before the picture (that exam keeps 1.2 in free).
- Otherwise centered between the question and the choices.
- A blank work grid (1.5 in) goes under the choices at the left.

**"Use this space for computations." box:** 1.23 × 0.4 in, gray line (#BFBFBF, 0.75 pt), no fill, 8 pt **bold**, centered,
not italic. **Decided:** it ends 0.25 in from the right edge of the paper (that exam: 0.17 in), on every
multiple-choice page. First MC page: level with the first question; following MC pages: just under the header line (0.83 in from the top).

## 7. Open response (Parts III and IV): borderless table

**Table:** no borders, at the left margin. That exam's is 7.12 in wide (0.12 in past the right margin); the app uses the full
7 in, so column 2 is 6.49 in.

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
|---|---|
| 0.51 in: the question number only | 6.61 in: everything else |

- One row per question; number in the same list, bold.
- Question text: 12 pt, single spaced.
- Credits `[2]`: own line, right aligned, regular weight, directly under the question text.
- Picture or grid: under the credit line at the left edge of column 2 (grids 2.9–3.2 in square); a construction picture is
  centered (4.8 × 4.4 in).
- One or two blank lines at the end of the row.
- Parts (a), (b), (c): bold `(a)`, 0.25 in hanging, one blank line between parts. The credit line is under the question,
  before (a); the grid is under the part that uses it.
- Two open questions fit on a page.

## 8. End of the test

- **Bonus. Decided:** the word *Bonus* bold italic at the left margin; bonus questions under it, not numbered, no table. Not
  counted in the score.
- **Score box. Decided:** always the bottom right corner of the last page, on a new page only if a question is in the way: 1.55 × 1.01 in, 0.5 pt black
  line, white. `Score:` 12 pt; under it `___/32`, 32 pt. Its right edge is 8 in from the left edge of the page.
- The total adds up the parts: 6 + 14 + 8 + 4 = 32.

## 9. What the app does differently today

| | Her exam | App now |
|---|---|---|
| Margins | 1 in top/bottom, 0.75 in sides | 0.5 in all round |
| Body line spacing | question text single, stacked and 2 × 2 choices 1.15 | 1.15 everywhere (her request 2026-11-23) |
| MC questions | borderless 0.4 + 5.9 in table | numbered paragraphs, full width |
| Open questions | borderless 0.51 + 6.61 in table | numbered paragraphs, full width |
| Computations box | right margin, gray, not italic, every MC page | inside the text, black, italic, only at the start of the part |
| Unit title | bold | italic |
| Teacher / calculator / time line | accommodated copy only | every copy, when filled in |
| Triangles | Δ | △ |
| Bonus | bold italic, not numbered | bold, numbered from 1 |
| Score box | fixed at the page corner, `___/32` at 32 pt | after the last question, `/32` at 26 pt, 1.9 × 1.2 in |
| Parts | new page each (checkbox, on to begin with) | run on |

## 10. Slips in that exam (not rules)

- Question 13: choice a starts lowercase, b–d with a capital.
- Question 12: `A(−5,3)` has no space after the comma; the other points do.
- Question 6 says "onto triangle ΔA′B′C′" (triangle twice).
