# Getting Started

## Open the app

Download `ALittleCandleTestBuilder.html` from the repository and open it in **Chrome** or **Edge**. Those two browsers print the page headers and footers correctly. No internet connection is needed.

Your work is saved automatically in that browser on that computer. Opening the file in a different browser, or a copy in another folder, starts with an empty bank. See [[Backups and Sync]].

## The three tabs

- **Question bank**: every question for the current course. Search, filter, edit and pick questions.
- **1 Pick questions → 2 Set up → 3 Print**: the three steps of making a test, on the rail at the left.
- **Question bank**: every question in the course, to edit, and **Your fields**.
- **Import & backup**: bring questions in, your banks, backups and courses.

The buttons across the top of each place (like **Spacing** or **Backup**) show one panel at a time.
**Next** at the bottom of each step moves you along, **Alt+1** to **Alt+3** jump to the steps; **« Icons only** at the bottom of the rail makes it narrow.

The **Course** menu at the top switches between courses. Each course has its own questions and settings.

## Help

Click **Help** at the top for a short guide inside the app. Its **Full user guide** link opens this wiki.

## Display settings

Click **Aa Display** at the top to make the app easier on your eyes:

- **Font**: Standard, **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (very clear letters) or **OpenDyslexic** (made for readers with dyslexia). Both are built in, so they work without the internet.
- **Text size**: Normal, Large or Larger.
- **High contrast**: darker text and stronger lines and outlines.
- **Less motion**: turns off animations. This also happens on its own if your computer is set to reduce motion.

These settings are just for you, in that browser. They don't change your printed tests, and a colleague's settings never change yours.

## Using the keyboard

- **Tab** moves between buttons and boxes, and a dark outline shows where you are.
- In the question editor, the row of math symbols is one Tab stop: press **Shift+Tab** from the question box to reach it, then use the **arrow keys** to pick a symbol and **Enter** to type it.
- **Ctrl+Enter** saves a question and **Esc** closes the editor (it asks first if you changed something).

## Your first document in five minutes

1. Pick your course from the **Course** menu.
2. On **Import & backup › Import**, under **ExamView banks**, choose a `.bnk` file.
3. In **1 Pick questions › Browse banks**, open a bank and a section, and tick the questions you want.
4. On **2 Set up › Document**, choose **Test**, **Quiz**, **Review** or **Lesson** and fill in the numbers (quarter, unit and so on).
5. Click **Print**. To make a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.
