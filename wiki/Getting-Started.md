# Getting Started

## Open the app

Download `ALittleCandleTestBuilder.html` from the repository and open it in **Chrome** or **Edge**. Those two browsers print the page headers and footers correctly. No internet connection is needed.

Your work is saved automatically in that browser on that computer. Opening the file in a different browser, or a copy in another folder, starts with an empty bank. See [[Backups and Sync]].

## The three tabs

- **Question bank**: every question for the current course. Search, filter, edit and pick questions.
- **Build & print**: choose the document type, fill in the details, see a preview and print.
- **Import & backup**: bring questions in, download or restore backups, and manage courses.

The **Course** menu at the top switches between courses. Each course has its own questions and settings.

## Your first document in five minutes

1. Pick your course from the **Course** menu.
2. On **Import & backup**, under **ExamView banks**, choose a `.bnk` file.
3. On **Question bank**, tick the questions you want.
4. On **Build & print**, choose **Test**, **Quiz**, **Review** or **Lesson** and fill in the numbers (quarter, unit and so on).
5. Click **Print**. To make a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.
