# Sharing with colleagues

On the **website version** (signed in), you and your colleagues can share question banks. The single file on your own
computer doesn't share; it stays a personal tool.

## Public and private

Every question you add is **private** at first: only you see it. You can make a bank **public**, so everyone with an
account on your website can use it.

- **Public** is for questions anyone may share, like **Regents questions**.
- **Private** is for questions you can't pass around, like a **textbook bank you bought**. Keep those private.

## Making a bank public

1. Open **Import & backup**.
2. Under **Your banks**, click **Make public** next to the bank.
3. Click again to confirm (**Confirm: everyone on this site sees it**).

The bank's questions get a **Public** tag in the question bank. To stop sharing, click **Public · make private**.
Your colleagues' copies disappear the next time their app syncs. **Undo** works right after either change.

You can also change one question: **Edit → More details → Who can see it**.

## Using a colleague's bank

1. Open **Import & backup** and find **Shared with you**. It lists every public bank, who shared it, and how many
   questions it has.
2. Tick the courses where you want to use it.

The questions now appear in your question bank for those courses, with a **Shared by …** tag. You can put them on tests
like your own questions. Your course choices follow you to your other computers.

Shared questions belong to the colleague who shared them, so only they can change them:

- **Copy to mine** makes your own private copy that you can edit freely.
- **Remove** takes the question out of the current course. It stays in **Shared with you**, so you can add it back.
- **Change these…** and **Check & clean up** leave shared questions alone.

## When someone leaves

If the admin removes an account, that person's public questions stay for everyone (the admin takes them over). Their
private questions are deleted with the account.
