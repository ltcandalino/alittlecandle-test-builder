# Courses

Each course (Geometry, Algebra 1, Algebra 2, Pre-Calculus, or any you add) has:
- its own questions
- its own **Build & print** settings: document type, numbers, fonts, selected questions

Switch courses with the **Course** menu at the top. Add, rename or delete courses on **Import & backup** under **Courses** (**Add course**, **Rename**, **Delete**).

When you import, questions go into the course that's currently chosen.
