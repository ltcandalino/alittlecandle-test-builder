# Building Documents

On **Build & print**, pick a document type at the top: **Test**, **Quiz**, **Review** or **Lesson**. Every page has the **course name** centered with a bar under it, in bold on the first page.

The **Course name** box sets that header. Leave it empty to use the course from the top menu.

## Test and Quiz

**Fill in:** Quarter #, Unit #, Unit title, Teacher, Calculator (Desmos, TI-84, No calculator, Scientific, or your own), Time accommodation (1.5×, 2.0× or a number of minutes).

**First page:**
- Name / Date line
- **Quarter 1 – Unit 3 Exam** (or **Quiz**) on the left, the *unit title* in italics on the right. Type in **Title line** to replace the automatic title.
- *Teacher*, *calculator* and *time accommodation* in italics

**Parts:** with **Group into parts** on, questions are grouped into Part I, Part II… with directions and a point total like **[14]**. You can edit the directions under **Part directions**. Options:
- "Use this space for computations." box at the start of each multiple-choice part
- Credits like **[2]** under each open-response question
- **Score box** at the end with the total, like **/33**

**Bonus questions:** click **bonus** next to a question in the list. Bonus questions print last under **Bonus**, numbered from 1, and don't count toward the score box.

**Footer:** "v. 1" (the version) on the first page, then page numbers.

## Review

**Fill in:** Unit #. The title is automatic (**Geometry Unit 3 Review**), or type your own.

- Questions are in a bordered table: **Short/Extended Response Questions** first, then **Multiple Choice Questions** (with a shaded purple heading row).
- Each question's source and exam (like "Regents June 2024 #12") prints in small gray type. Turn this off with **Show the reference under each question**.
- **Work space** sets how much blank room goes under each short/extended response question: each question's own setting, none, or ½ inch up to 4 inches.
- Pages 2 and on show *Unit 3 Review* under the bar.

## Lesson

**Fill in:**
- **Unit #** and **Section #**: print as **3-4** under the header
- **Quarter #, Week #, Day #**: make the footer code, like **Q1W03D012**
- **Source**: printed in the footer. If it's empty, the questions' sources are used.
- **I am learning to…**: the learning target
- **Essential Concept**: a topic and a definition, printed in a box. Leave both empty to skip it.
- **Notes**: optional text plus blank space (in inches)
- **Success Criteria**: one "I can…" statement per line, printed as bullets

**Students need:** tick **Compass**, **Ruler** and/or **Tracing paper** to show those pictures in the top-left corner of the first page.

**Question roles:** in the list on the right, set each question to **Warm Up**, **Example** or **Practice**. Examples print as *Example 1, Example 2…* with the concept name you type under each one. Practice questions are numbered.

**Order on the page:** Warm Up! → Notes → Essential Concept → Examples → Success Criteria → Practice.

**Footer (pages 2+):** the Q#W##D### code and *Source:* on the left, the page number on the right.

## Settings for every type

The settings you rarely change are folded under **Page style** at the bottom of the Document box. Click it to open it; it stays open or closed the way you left it.

- **Font** and **Size** (Century Schoolbook 12 pt by default)
- **Answer choice labels**: a. b. c. d., A. B. C. D., a) b) c) d), or (1) (2) (3) (4) Regents style
- **Name and date line**
- **Answer key** options: show unit and standards, show your notes

Settings are saved per course, so Geometry and Algebra 1 each remember their own.
