# ALittleCandleTestBuilder

Build printed **tests, quizzes, reviews and lessons** for high school math from your own question banks: ExamView `.bnk` files, PDFs, Word documents, pasted text or a spreadsheet.

## Start here

1. [[Getting Started]]: open the app and import your first bank
2. [[Importing Questions]]: `.bnk`, PDF, Word, paste and CSV
3. [[Question Bank]]: editing questions and their details
4. [[Building Documents]]: Test, Quiz, Review and Lesson
5. [[Printing and Answer Keys]]: versions, scrambling, PDFs
6. [[Courses]]: Geometry, Algebra 1, Algebra 2, Pre-Calculus and more
7. [[Backups and Sync]]: keeping your work safe and moving it between computers
8. [[Website Setup (Docker)]]: running it as a website with logins for colleagues
9. [[Troubleshooting]]

## Two ways to use it

| | Single file | Website (Docker) |
|---|---|---|
| How | Open `ALittleCandleTestBuilder.html` in Chrome or Edge | Run the Docker container on your web hosting or VPS |
| Logins | None | Separate login for you and each colleague |
| Where your work is saved | That browser on that computer | Your server, synced to any computer you sign in on |
| Setup | None | About 15 minutes |
