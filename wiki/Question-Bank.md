# Question Bank

**Question bank** (on the rail) lists every question in the current course, with everything you need to look after them: **Edit**,
**Copy**, **Delete**, **Change these…**, **Check & clean up** and **+ New question**. **1 Pick questions** is only for picking:
its lists have the tick box and **Edit** (and **Copy to mine** on a colleague's question), so a question can only be deleted here in the Question bank.

## Browse banks

**Browse banks** is the last tab on **1 Pick questions** (**Search** opens first). Inside a colleague's bank or a team bank, **Copy bank to mine** makes your own copy of the whole bank.

**1 Pick questions › Browse banks** opens with one card per bank (your banks, every bank a colleague made public, and team banks).
The **Course** menu at the top chooses which course's banks show: this course to begin with, another of your courses, or **Any course**.

1. Click a bank to see its **units** and **sections**, with how many questions each has and how many are already on the test.
2. Click a unit (all of it) or a section. Its questions show with the tick box and **Edit**.
3. Click a question (anywhere on it) to put it on the test, and click again to take it off. **Shift-click** another question to add every one in between. Or or use **Add 5 at random** for that section (**Undo** takes them off again).
4. The path at the top (**All banks › NY Regents Geometry › Unit 3 · Section 2**) goes back. **Set them up** jumps to **2 Set up**.

A bank without units or sections opens straight to its questions. Browsing never changes your **Search** filters.

A question taught in more than one unit has its units separated by commas, like **10, 11**. It shows under each of those units when you browse or filter by unit, and its card says **Units 10, 11**.

## Finding questions

On **1 Pick questions**, the **Course** menu right after Search starts on your document's course. Choose another of your courses, or
**Any course**, to find questions from banks that fit several courses (like older Math A banks); you can put them on your document, and a
question from another course shows that course's name on its card.

Search by words, or filter by **unit**, **section**, **topic** and **subtopic**. Click **More filters** for type, month, year, cluster, standard, bank, source and your own fields; the button shows how many of those are in use. On **Question bank**, More filters also has **Needs attention** (it isn't on 1 Pick questions).

The filters narrow each other: after you pick **Unit 4**, the other menus only list sections, topics and standards that Unit 4's questions have. What you already picked always stays in its menu.

Every filter you're using shows as a chip under the filters, like **Unit: 3 ✕**. Click a chip to remove that filter, or **Clear filters** to remove them all. **Sort** (next to "Showing … of …") puts the list in bank order, newest added first, by exam date (newest or oldest first), by standard, or by unit and section.

Tick the questions you want in your document. **Select all** puts every question shown (all the matches, not just the first page) on the test; **Deselect all** takes them off. Both have **Undo**. They're added to the list on **2 Set up › Questions** in the order you pick them. Each card shows its unit and section as **Unit 3 · Sec 2**, and a ticked card has a purple outline.

## Editing a question

Click a question to open it. You can fix the wording, choices or answer, and fill in its details:

| Detail | Example |
|---|---|
| Course, Bank, Source | Geometry · GEO Regents · Regents |
| Unit, Section | 3 · 2 (several units: 10, 11) |
| Part | Part III (used when grouping a test into parts) |
| Topic, Subtopic | Triangles · Congruence |
| Standard, Cluster | G.SRT.B.5 · G-SRT.B |
| Old NY standard | A.A.7 (the 2005 NY code some ExamView banks list next to the Common Core one) |
| Your own fields | DOK 2, Difficulty: hard… (see below) |
| Reference | `062412` → June 2024, #12 |
| Points | 2 |
| Notes | Your own notes. They print only on answer keys, never on student copies. |

Use **+ New question** to write one from scratch, or **Save & add another** to enter several in a row.

The editor shows the type, points, unit, section and standard first, then the question box, where the cursor starts at the end of the text, then the answers. Bank, topic, part, subtopic, cluster, your notes, reference, source and exam month/year/question # are under **More details**. It opens by itself when a question has any of them.

The symbol buttons above the question box type math symbols: ∠ △ ≅ ∼ ⊥ ∥ ° π √ ≠ ≤ ≥ ± × ÷ θ ′, the arrow → and the composition symbol ∘ (as in f ∘ g), then marks for segments, lines, rays and arcs, and x² / x₂.

If you press **Esc**, click **Cancel** or click outside the editor after changing something, it asks first: **Keep editing** or **Discard changes**. Nothing you typed is thrown away without asking.

## Changing many questions at once

Filter the list down to the questions you want, then click **Change these …** (next to "Showing … of …").

1. Pick the detail: unit, section, topic, subtopic, standard, old NY standard, cluster, bank, source, part, points, notes, or one of your own fields.
2. Pick how: **set to** a value, **add** a value (a standard is added to the list, like `A.REI.B.4, A.SSE.A.1`), **find and replace** some text, or **clear it**.
3. Click **Apply**. If some of the shown questions are ticked, you can choose **only on the ticked** ones.

**Undo** puts everything back right after.

## Check & clean up

**Check & clean up** (top right of the Question bank) looks through every question in the course:

| It finds | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Old NY codes mixed into the standard, like `A.REI.A.1, NY: A.N.1` | **Move them to "Old NY standard"**: Standard keeps `A.REI.A.1`, and `A.N.1` goes to its own field |
| A standard but no cluster | **Fill in the clusters** (A.REI.B.4 → A.REI.B) |
| No unit or section, when every other question with the same standard has the same one | **Copy unit and section** |
| No correct answer | **Show them**, then open each and click the right answer |
| No standard, no unit, no section | **Show them**, then use **Change these …** |

**Show them** sets the **Needs attention** filter, so the list shows only those questions. Every fix can be undone.

## Your own fields

Add details the banks don't have, like DOK or difficulty, under **Question bank → Your fields**, or with **+ Add a field** at the bottom of **More details** in the editor. Then:

- fill them in under **More details**, or for many questions at once with **Change these …**
- filter by them under **More filters**
- they're in backups and bank files

Rename or delete a field on **Question bank → Your fields**. Deleting removes it from every question (with Undo).

**Hide** keeps a field's values in your questions, backups and bank files but shows it nowhere in the app: no filter, not in the editor,
not on the cards. **Show** brings it back. The JMAP topic and JMAP subtopic fields in the Regents bank files come hidden.

## Question types

| Type | What prints |
|---|---|
| Multiple choice | The question and its choices |
| Bimodal | A multiple-choice question that can also be asked without choices (see below) |
| True/False | T and F to circle |
| Short answer | Answer lines |
| Extended response | Lines or blank work space (you choose how much) |

**Bimodal:** on **2 Set up › Spacing**, choose whether bimodal questions print *with* their choices or *without* them (as open-ended questions with work space). You can switch single questions in the list on the right.

## Deleting

Deleted questions can be brought back with **Undo** right after you delete them.
