# Question Bank

The **Question bank** tab lists every question in the current course.

## Finding questions

Search by words, then filter by bank, source, topic, subtopic, cluster, month, year and more, and sort the list. Tick the questions you want in your document. They're added to the list on **Build & print** in the order you pick them.

## Editing a question

Click a question to open it. You can fix the wording, choices or answer, and fill in its details:

| Detail | Example |
|---|---|
| Course, Bank, Source | Geometry · GEO Regents · Regents |
| Unit, Section | 3 · 2 |
| Part | Part III (used when grouping a test into parts) |
| Topic, Subtopic | Triangles · Congruence |
| Standard, Cluster | G.SRT.B.5 · G-SRT.B |
| Reference | `062412` → June 2024, #12 |
| Points | 2 |
| Notes | Your own notes. They print only on answer keys, never on student copies. |

Use **+ New question** to write one from scratch, or **Save & add another** to enter several in a row.

## Question types

| Type | What prints |
|---|---|
| Multiple choice | The question and its choices |
| Bimodal | A multiple-choice question that can also be asked without choices (see below) |
| True/False | T and F to circle |
| Short answer | Answer lines |
| Extended response | Lines or blank work space (you choose how much) |

**Bimodal:** on **Build & print**, choose whether bimodal questions print *with* their choices or *without* them (as open-ended questions with work space). You can switch single questions in the list on the right.

## Deleting

Deleted questions can be brought back with **Undo** right after you delete them.
