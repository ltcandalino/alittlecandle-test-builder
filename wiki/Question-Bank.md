# Question Bank

The **Question bank** tab lists every question in the current course.

## Finding questions

Search by words, or filter by **unit**, **section**, **topic** and **standard**. Click **More filters** for needs attention, bank, type, year, month, cluster, subtopic, source and your own fields; the button shows how many of those are in use.

Every filter you're using shows as a chip under the filters, like **Unit: 3 ✕**. Click a chip to remove that filter, or **Clear filters** to remove them all. **Sort** (next to "Showing … of …") puts the list in bank order, newest added first, by exam date (newest or oldest first), by standard, or by unit and section.

Tick the questions you want in your document. They're added to the list on **Build & print** in the order you pick them. Each card shows its unit and section as **Unit 3 · Sec 2**, and a ticked card has a teal outline.

## Editing a question

Click a question to open it. You can fix the wording, choices or answer, and fill in its details:

| Detail | Example |
|---|---|
| Course, Bank, Source | Geometry · GEO Regents · Regents |
| Unit, Section | 3 · 2 |
| Part | Part III (used when grouping a test into parts) |
| Topic, Subtopic | Triangles · Congruence |
| Standard, Cluster | G.SRT.B.5 · G-SRT.B |
| Old NY standard | A.A.7 (the 2005 NY code some ExamView banks list next to the Common Core one) |
| Your own fields | DOK 2, Difficulty: hard… (see below) |
| Reference | `062412` → June 2024, #12 |
| Points | 2 |
| Notes | Your own notes. They print only on answer keys, never on student copies. |

Use **+ New question** to write one from scratch, or **Save & add another** to enter several in a row.

The editor shows the type, points, unit, section and standard first, then the question box, where the cursor starts at the end of the text, then the answers. Bank, topic, part, subtopic, cluster, your notes, reference, source and exam month/year/question # are under **More details**. It opens by itself when a question has any of them.

If you press **Esc**, click **Cancel** or click outside the editor after changing something, it asks first: **Keep editing** or **Discard changes**. Nothing you typed is thrown away without asking.

## Changing many questions at once

Filter the list down to the questions you want, then click **Change these …** (next to "Showing … of …").

1. Pick the detail: unit, section, topic, subtopic, standard, old NY standard, cluster, bank, source, part, points, notes, or one of your own fields.
2. Pick how: **set to** a value, **add** a value (a standard is added to the list, like `A.REI.B.4, A.SSE.A.1`), **find and replace** some text, or **clear it**.
3. Click **Apply**. If some of the shown questions are ticked, you can choose **only on the ticked** ones.

**Undo** puts everything back right after.

## Check & clean up

**Check & clean up** (top right of the Question bank) looks through every question in the course:

| It finds | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Old NY codes mixed into the standard, like `A.REI.A.1, NY: A.N.1` | **Move them to "Old NY standard"**: Standard keeps `A.REI.A.1`, and `A.N.1` goes to its own field |
| A standard but no cluster | **Fill in the clusters** (A.REI.B.4 → A.REI.B) |
| No unit or section, when every other question with the same standard has the same one | **Copy unit and section** |
| No correct answer | **Show them**, then open each and click the right answer |
| No standard, no unit, no section | **Show them**, then use **Change these …** |

**Show them** sets the **Needs attention** filter, so the list shows only those questions. Every fix can be undone.

## Your own fields

Add details the banks don't have, like DOK or difficulty, under **Import & backup → Your own fields**, or with **+ Add a field** at the bottom of **More details** in the editor. Then:

- fill them in under **More details**, or for many questions at once with **Change these …**
- filter by them under **More filters**
- they're columns in CSV files, and they're in backups and bank files

Rename or delete a field on **Import & backup**. Deleting removes it from every question (with Undo).

## Question types

| Type | What prints |
|---|---|
| Multiple choice | The question and its choices |
| Bimodal | A multiple-choice question that can also be asked without choices (see below) |
| True/False | T and F to circle |
| Short answer | Answer lines |
| Extended response | Lines or blank work space (you choose how much) |

**Bimodal:** on **Build & print**, choose whether bimodal questions print *with* their choices or *without* them (as open-ended questions with work space). You can switch single questions in the list on the right.

## Deleting

Deleted questions can be brought back with **Undo** right after you delete them.
