# Printing and Answer Keys

## Versions and scrambling

Choose 1 to 8 **Versions** (A–H) on **Build & print**.

- **Shuffle question order**: within each part or section
- **Shuffle answer choices**: "All of the above" and "None of the above" stay last
- **Keep Version A in the original order**: handy as your master copy
- **New scramble**: makes a new shuffle. Versions stay the same until you press it, so an answer key always matches copies you've already printed.

## Printing

- **Print … + answer keys**, **… only** or **Answer keys only**
- The browser's print window opens. To make a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.
- **Print each version separately**: each version starts at page 1 with its own first-page header. The browser opens one print window per version.

Use **Chrome** or **Edge** (version 131 or newer). Other browsers may leave out the page headers and footers.

## Answer keys

Each version gets its own key, listing every question's answer in that version's order. Bonus questions are listed as *Bonus 1, Bonus 2…* and lesson questions as *Warm Up*, *Example 1*… Options:
- **Show unit and standards on the answer key**
- **Show my notes on the answer key** (notes never print on student copies)

## Preview

The preview on **Build & print** updates as you change settings. Use **Show answers** to preview the key marks and **A / B / C…** to look at each version.
